Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026. The Norwegian version is the authoritative one.

Who is responsible

LiT Partners, Storgata 22, Sandnes, Norway, is the controller of the personal data collected on this website and in our work on EPD packs. Send questions about privacy to litpartners24@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • When you fill in the form: your name, company, email address, phone number if you give it, the options you choose and what you write in the message field.
  • How you found us: campaign information in the link you arrived from, such as the name of an ad, and which page linked to us. This is sent with the form. Nothing is stored in your browser.
  • When you order an EPD pack: the files you send us, such as bills of materials and requirement texts, and any contact details in them.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • To reply to and follow up your enquiry: steps before a possible contract, and our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us (GDPR Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).
  • To prepare and deliver the EPD pack: our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To see which campaigns bring enquiries: our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)). We only look at the number of enquiries per campaign.

We do not use your details for newsletters or email marketing unless you have agreed to it.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, analytics tools or tracking pixels, and stores nothing in your browser. The fonts are hosted on this website, so the page loads nothing from other websites.

Who processes data on our behalf

  • The form: FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives the form and forwards it to our email.
  • The website: GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts the website on GitHub Pages and may record your IP address in server logs for security purposes.
  • Email: Google (Gmail), where enquiries arrive.

We do not sell personal data or share it with anyone else.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract are deleted after 12 months.
  • Files you send for an EPD pack are deleted no later than 90 days after delivery, unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Data we need for our accounts is kept for as long as the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct, delete or restrict it. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest, and ask to receive your data. Write to litpartners24@gmail.com. If you believe we process personal data unlawfully, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.
