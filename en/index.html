EPD requirements are getting stricter, and we are here to help

Finding an EPD is not the hard part. The hard part is knowing it is the right EPD, that it is valid on the delivery date, and that it meets exactly what the client asked for.

Grid companies

EPDs for a large share of the delivery

Under a joint requirement from Norway's largest grid companies, from 1 January 2025 a supplier must be able to document EPDs for grid components covering at least 80 % of the material value of the contract. There are exceptions, including contracts below the EEA threshold.

Sources: the grid companies' joint requirement and Eidsiva's supplier requirements.

Public procurement

Climate and environment count in the evaluation

In Norwegian tenders subject to the announcement duty, where award criteria are weighted, climate and environment must carry at least 30 % of the weight. The client may instead set environmental requirements in the specification, if that gives a better effect and is justified in the tender documents.

Source: DFØ's guidance to the Procurement Act.

The wrong EPD

Right product, wrong document

An EPD that has expired, covers a different product or follows the older standard (EN 15804+A1) can be rejected, even when the product is the right one.

Questions and answers

What is an EPD?

An Environmental Product Declaration is a third-party verified document describing a product's environmental impact over its life cycle, including greenhouse gas emissions. For construction products, EPDs follow the EN 15804 standard. For electrical and electronic products, PEP ecopassport (EN 50693) is also used.

Which products can you handle?

Electrical and E&I products: cables, cable management, switchboards and enclosures, protective devices, lighting and similar. If we cannot find a valid EPD, the line is marked missing, and we contact the supplier for you.

What do you need from us?

The bill of materials from your estimate (Excel or CSV, with product numbers where you have them), the client's EPD requirements (for example the requirement text from the tender documents), and the delivery date if you have it. With unit prices, we can also show coverage by material value.

How long does it take?

Two working days from when we have the bill of materials and the requirements. If it is urgent, say so in the form.

What happens to our files and prices?

We use your files only to prepare your pack, never share them, and delete them no later than 90 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them. Prices are not shown in the report.

Is this a certification?

No. We check the EPD documentation against the requirements the client has set. We do not confirm that the project complies with laws or regulations.

Do you work in English?

Yes. We can reply and prepare the pack in English or Norwegian.
